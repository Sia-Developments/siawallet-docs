# Where to Buy Siacoin in 2026 (Exchanges, Not Coinbase/Binance/Kraken)

If you've searched for "buy Siacoin" and immediately checked Coinbase, you already hit the first surprise: it isn't there. Here's the actual state of Siacoin's exchange availability, and what to do with it once you have it.

## Where Siacoin does — and doesn't — trade

Siacoin trades on roughly 60 cryptocurrency exchanges as of 2026, but three of the largest US-facing platforms aren't among them:

- **Coinbase** does not list Siacoin.
- **Kraken** does not list Siacoin.
- **Binance** delisted Siacoin in 2021.

Current US-accessible venues include **KuCoin**, **Gate.io**, **MEXC**, and **HTX**. Instant-swap services like **LetsExchange** and **Godex** are also options if you're converting from another coin rather than depositing fiat. Exchange availability by jurisdiction changes over time, so it's worth checking each platform's own policy before attempting a deposit rather than assuming a listing is universally accessible.

This absence of tier-1 US exchange listings is a genuine limitation — it makes price discovery thinner and on/off-ramping less convenient for US users than for coins with Coinbase-grade liquidity — but it isn't evidence the network itself is inactive. Sixty exchange listings is a long tail of mid-tier venues, not zero.

## A quick note on price and predictions

Siacoin's historical all-time high was around $0.11, not anywhere near $1 — treat any "Siacoin to $1" prediction you see with real skepticism, since neither the Sia Foundation nor this project publishes price targets. Siacoin is a utility token used to pay for decentralized storage, and its price is driven by storage demand rather than the kind of narrative-driven speculation that moves larger-cap coins. For actual analysis rather than price talk, the Sia Foundation's quarterly "State of Sia" reports are the more useful read.

## Watch out for lookalike tokens and fake listings

Small-cap coins with recognizable names attract impersonators, and Siacoin is no exception. Before depositing on any exchange, double-check three things: the contract address or asset identifier matches Siacoin's actual network (Siacoin is not an ERC-20 or BEP-20 token — it runs on its own blockchain, so any "Siacoin" token living on Ethereum or BNB Chain is not the real asset); the exchange listing page links back to verifiable, consistent information rather than a freshly created listing with no history; and any link you're following to reach the exchange came from the exchange's own official domain, not a search ad or a link posted in a Telegram or Discord channel. Scam tokens riding on a real project's name are a recurring pattern across the entire small-cap crypto space, and Siacoin's absence from Coinbase and Kraken — while a genuine liquidity limitation — also makes it an easier target for impersonation, since there's no single obvious "official" venue to point people toward.

If you're ever unsure whether a specific exchange listing is legitimate, cross-reference it against a well-established aggregator like CoinGecko, which tracks verified markets for Siacoin across its listed exchanges. A "Siacoin" pair that doesn't show up there at all is a reason to pause before depositing.

## What to do after you buy

This is the step a lot of buying guides skip, and it's the more important one. Exchanges are custodial — what you hold there is an IOU, not the coins themselves, and their security and solvency are entirely outside your control. If your Siacoin isn't actively part of a trade you're making right now, the sensible move is to withdraw it to a non-custodial wallet you control.

That means:

1. [Install a non-custodial Sia wallet](how-to-set-up-sia-wallet-first-time.md).
2. Generate a receive address and copy it carefully.
3. Send a small test amount first — a few dollars' worth — and confirm it arrives before moving the rest.
4. Withdraw the remainder once the test transaction is confirmed.
5. Back up your seed phrase in two physically separate locations, written on paper — never as a screenshot, cloud note, or password manager entry.

For balances you plan to hold for a while rather than actively trade, [pairing a software wallet with a Ledger hardware wallet](connect-ledger-to-siacoin-wallet.md) is the common setup for serious long-term storage.
